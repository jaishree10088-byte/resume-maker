AI Writing Help, Honestly Explained

The builder offers template-based writing suggestions built from your own words — for example, turning a short note about a job into a clear, well-worded bullet point. Every suggestion is shown to you first and is fully editable.

It never invents qualifications, jobs or skills you do not have, and it never sends your details anywhere: the suggestions work from what you typed, in your browser. What you approve is what appears on your resume — nothing more.
